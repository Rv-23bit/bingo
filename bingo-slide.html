How It Works

📱
Step 1

Open the digital bingo card on your phone — no paper needed!

🗣️
Step 2

Walk around and talk to people. Find someone who matches a square.

✅
Step 3

Tap to mark the square. You can't use the same person twice!

🏆
Step 4

First to get 5 in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) shouts "BINGO!"

🎯 Your Bingo Card

Facilitator Pro Tips

⏱️

Set a Timer

Project a 15-minute countdown. If nobody wins, switch to "most squares filled" for the win.

🎵

Play Background Music

Upbeat lo-fi or pop keeps energy high and reduces awkward silences.

🎁

Have Prizes Ready

Even small rewards (candy, stickers, bragging rights) boost engagement massively.

🔄

Shuffle the Cards

Everyone gets a randomized card so the same person can't solve all boards at once.

📣

Demo First

Walk up to someone live and demo a square check-off — modeling removes hesitation.

🧩

Backup Plan

If the group is small (< 12), switch to a 4×4 grid or allow two squares per person.
